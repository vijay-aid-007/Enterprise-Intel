# XGBoost tuning experiment

Generated by `python -m src.ml.tune_report` from `models/tuning.json`. Do not edit by hand.

**Outcome: the baseline was kept.**

## Protocol (fixed before the first run)

- Training split only (5634 customers). The test split plays no part in the search or the decision.
- 40 seeded random XGBoost configurations plus the baseline, scored with repeated stratified 5-fold cross-validation (2 repeats) on the same folds, so scores are paired.
- The best 3 by mean PR-AUC and the baseline were re-scored on fresh folds (3 repeats).
- Adoption needs all of: a mean PR-AUC gain of at least 0.005; a corrected one-sided paired t-test below 0.05 divided by 3; and a mean Brier score no worse by more than 0.002.
- No early stopping, because it would score each round on the validation fold.

## How to read this

- PR-AUC measures how well customers are ranked. Brier measures probability error and is sensitive to class weighting, so a Brier gain here does not by itself mean better ranking.
- Folds of repeated cross-validation share training rows. The Nadeau-Bengio correction accounts for that approximately, so the p-value is a guide, not a proof.
- Picking the best of 3 finalists on the same folds is why the significance level is divided by 3.
- Run time: 67.2 s.
